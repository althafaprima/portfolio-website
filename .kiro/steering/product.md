# Product

A personal portfolio website for Althaf Aprima Ksatria, a Business Analyst / Information Systems Management professional (Master's, Summa Cum Laude) based in Jakarta, Indonesia.

Audience: recruiters and hiring managers for Business Analyst, Data, and Product roles. Secondary: collaborators and lecturers.

Goal: within 30 seconds a visitor knows who he is, what he has done (with proof), and how to contact him or download his CV.

Voice: first person ("I"), professional, clear, confident, never exaggerated. Language: English.

Hard rule: never invent facts, numbers, metrics, testimonials, clients, or links. Unknown information becomes a visible placeholder starting with "TODO:".
